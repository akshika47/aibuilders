About CAL AI

What is CAL AI?

CAL AI stands for Calorie Analysis with AI. It uses advanced artificial intelligence to analyze food images and provide detailed nutritional information.

How to get the best results:

  • Take clear, well-lit photos of your food
  • Include the entire meal or dish in the frame
  • Avoid blurry or dark images
  • For best accuracy, photograph food on a plain background

What you'll get:

  • Identification of food items and ingredients
  • Estimated nutritional values (calories, protein, carbs, fat, etc.)
  • Health insights and dietary recommendations
  • Portion size estimates

Note: Nutritional values are estimates based on visual analysis. For precise dietary tracking, consult nutrition labels or a registered dietitian.
